**Nest effects** — close inner tags first, same as colors:

`{gold}{glow}shiny gold{/}{/}`

`{size:2}` … `{/}` makes inline text bigger (owners can use `{size:3}` up to `{size:8}`). `{big}` and `{huge}` work too.

`@name` pings that person if they are online. `@here` pings everyone in the chamber. They can mute the sound in Account settings.

Hover your message and press **✎** to edit it. Regular members show **edited** — click it to see the old text. Owner edits stay quiet (no edited tag).

### Staff (needs the rank ability)

Times: `30s` to `7d` — examples `5m` `10m` `30m` `1h` `6h` `1d`

/mute username 10m being loud
/unmute username

/restrict username main-vault 1h cool down
/unrestrict username

- **Mute** — they can’t talk or play effects in chambers
- **Restrict** — they can only see that one chamber until the time runs out
- You cannot mute/restrict the owner, or yourself
- Same controls live on a person’s ⚙ settings

Grant these on a rank in Settings → Ranks:

- Mute people
- Lock to a chamber
